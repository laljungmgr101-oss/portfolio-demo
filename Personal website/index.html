About me

Curious mind, shipping code

I'm a BCA undergrad who fell for the web the moment a <div> changed colour. Since then I've been building interfaces, breaking them, and learning why they broke.

I study Computer Applications (BCA) and spend most of my free time turning ideas into small, polished products. I care about the details other people skip — keyboard focus states, loading skeletons, copy that actually sounds human.

My sweet spot is the front end: React, CSS and a stubborn refusal to ship something that feels slow. On the other side of the stack I'm comfortable with Node, REST APIs and SQL — enough to build a thing end-to-end without waiting for a backend dev.

When I'm not coding you'll find me in a hackathon, sketching UI in Figma, or explaining to my friends why their laptop needs more RAM.
